💡 Problem

Improper waste segregation remains a major challenge because people often:

Don't know how to categorize unfamiliar waste
Are unsure about proper disposal methods
Lack incentives to consistently segregate waste
Have little visibility into their individual environmental impact

At the municipal level, fragmented information can also make monitoring and collection planning more difficult.

💚 Our Solution

BinThere combines AI-powered waste identification, gamification and community-level engagement into one platform.

Users can:

📸 Scan a waste item
🤖 Identify its category using AI
♻️ Learn how to dispose of it
🏆 Earn points for responsible disposal
👥 Compete through individual and building leaderboards
📊 Track their recycling history and impact

Municipal authorities can access aggregated information through the municipal dashboard.

✨ Key Features
🤖 AI Waste Identification
Classifies waste from an uploaded image and provides disposal guidance.
🏆 Gamified Leaderboards
Users and buildings compete to encourage sustained participation.
🎁 Rewards System
Users earn points through responsible waste segregation.
🌐 Multilingual Interface
Supports English, বাংলা and हिंदी.
📊 Personal Waste History
Users can track their previous scans, points and estimated recycling contribution.
🏛️ Municipal Dashboard
Provides waste, participation and pickup-related insights.
👥 Community Engagement
Building-level participation turns individual actions into a collective challenge.
